ABOUT THE PROGRAM

IDEAS START SMALL.
RECOGNITION SHOULDN'T.

CREVA is a creative recognition platform celebrating original ideas, creative projects, emerging businesses and experiences that demonstrate vision, originality and meaningful execution.

CREVA is open to individuals, teams, startups, businesses, designers, creators and innovators — anyone building something worth a closer look.

WHAT WE RECOGNIZE

Five ways an idea can stand out.

01

Creative Experience

Experiences, activations, installations, events and new ways of engaging people.

02

Physical products, objects, packaging and design-led solutions.

03

Emerging Venture

Startups, businesses and new commercial concepts.

04

Creative Technology

Technology used to create new products, experiences or solutions.

05

Visionary Concept

Early-stage ideas and concepts with strong creative potential.

WHY CREVA?

An open place for original ideas.

CREVA was created to give original ideas a place to be seen and recognized.

We believe creativity is not limited to one industry or one medium. It can take the form of a business, a product, an experience, a technology, a design, or an idea that challenges the expected.

CREVA brings these ideas together and recognizes the people behind them.
